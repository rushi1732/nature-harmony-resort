## Key Website Components
- **Navbar**: Sticky transparent-to-solid navigation with brand emblem, menu links, and "Book Your Stay" CTA.
- **Hero**: Full-screen cinematic hero featuring authentic resort photograph at twilight (`photo_25.jpg`), asymmetrical typography, Google rating badge, and "Explore The Resort" / "Plan Your Stay" CTAs.
- **QuickBookingBar**: Floating booking bar with Check-in date, Check-out date, Guest counter, Stay Type selector, and quick availability check.
- **IntroductionSection**: Editorial asymmetric layout introducing the hosts (Dr. Ravrane, Dr. Ma'am), the Konkani heritage, and layered photographs.
- **HighlightsStrip**: Organic wave banner with 4 circular badges (Nature Surroundings, Comfortable Stay, Delicious Home Food, Personalized Care) and a tilted taped Polaroid photo with handwritten note *"Feel the Nature..."*.
- **ServicesSection**: Interactive cards for all 8 authentic services with real resort imagery, badges, detail drawers, and direct WhatsApp enquiry buttons.
- **ExperiencesSection**: 4 editorial pillars covering Konkani Home Feasts, Covered Swimming Pool, Evening Gazebo with Dr. Ravrane's Poetry, and Nature Trails.
- **GallerySection**: Categorized scrapbook photo gallery (All, Cottages, Pool & Nature, Dining & Events) with horizontal filmstrip, zoom hover, and full-screen keyboard/touch accessible lightbox modal.
- **ReviewsSection**: Genuine Google guest testimonials highlighting the hospitality, the food (fresh Ghawane, fish thali), and the atmosphere, with 4.7★ rating.
- **LocationSection**: Precise location information on Chiplun-Guhagar Road, distance guides (Guhagar Beach ~30 min, Chiplun ~20 min), interactive embedded Google Map, and "Get Directions" trigger.
- **BookingModal**: Complete stay enquiry modal with stay duration calculation, real-time input validation, and automatic formatted WhatsApp message generator targeting `+91 7666173397`.
- **Footer**: Forest green footer with logo, taglines, quick links, verified services, and contact info.

## Tech Stack
- React 18 + TypeScript + Vite
- Tailwind CSS with custom brand theme tokens
- Framer Motion for subtle, high-end transitions
- Lucide React for consistent icons
- High-resolution Google CDN image optimization (`getOptimizedPhotoUrl`)
